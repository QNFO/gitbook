# About QNFO

**QNFO** (pronounced "info") is a scientific research incubator advancing knowledge 
for the collective benefit of all. QNFO is the research identity of Empowering Change (501c3).

## Mission

To develop and openly publish foundational research in ultrametric quantum computing,
p-adic physics, and glass-box artificial intelligence — replacing Archimedean (continuous) 
geometry with ultrametric (tree-based) geometry for computation and intelligence.

## Key Research Areas

- **Ultrametric Quantum Computing (QWAV):** Passive fault tolerance through the strong 
  triangle inequality — error suppression without active correction
- **Quantum-Native p-Adic Neural Architectures (Q-PNA):** Glass-box AI on tree topologies 
  with auditable attention
- **Adelic Physics:** Unifying number-theoretic geometry with physical law
- **Bruhat-Tits Geometry:** The tree as a unifying geometric object

## Publications

All research is published open-access via:
- **GitBook** (this site) — multilingual research documentation
- **Zenodo** — archival DOI-registered publications
- **GitHub** — code, interactive demos, and technical specifications
